# Chatting

- **Start a chat.** Type in the box at the bottom and press **Return**. Press **Shift Return** for a new line. **⌘K** starts a new chat.
- **Stop a reply** with the stop button or **Esc**.
- **Edit or retry.** Hover over your message and click the pencil to change it, or use the retry button under a reply. Every version is kept: switch between them with the **‹ 1/2 ›** arrows.
- **Think harder.** The brain button asks for a more careful, step-by-step answer, which is useful for math and tricky questions.
- **Incognito chats** (in the sidebar) are never saved and never read or write memory.
- **Find old chats** with the search box in the sidebar. It searches inside messages too.
- **Export** a chat as Markdown, JSON or PDF with the download button at the top right.
- **Light or dark.** Pick a theme at the bottom of the sidebar, or follow your Mac's setting.

Press **?** to see every keyboard shortcut.

{: .tip }
Replies can be wrong. For facts that matter, turn on [web search](web) so the answer cites its sources, and check them.
