# Voice

There are three ways to use your voice:

- **Talk** (the headphones button, or **⌘⇧V**) starts a spoken conversation. Speak naturally; it answers out loud, usually within a second. Interrupt it any time by speaking, or press **Space**. Click **End** when you're done. The conversation is saved as a chat.
- **Dictate** with the microphone button: your words appear in the message box as you speak, so you can check them before sending. You can also hold the **Right Option** key anywhere in the app.
- **Voice messages** (the waveform button) are recorded and sent as audio, so the assistant hears *how* you said it, not just the words.

If it interrupts itself, the microphone is probably hearing the speakers. Use headphones or lower the volume.
